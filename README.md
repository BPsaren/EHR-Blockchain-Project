# DER REKORSMIN Project

Introduction
An Electronic Health Record (EHR) is a digital version of a patient’s medical file that
compiles a wide spectrum of healthcare data. It includes vital elements such as
demographic details, clinical issues, medication history, physician assessments, vital signs,
past medical records, immunizations, lab results, radiology reports, personal identifiers,
progress updates, and billing records.
Designed to manage complex medical information efficiently, EHR systems automate data
handling and streamline clinical workflows. They create a comprehensive view of a
patient’s medical interactions and support functions like evidence-based decision-making,
quality control, and outcome tracking.
EHRs are versatile tools that cater to different roles within the healthcare system:
• Administrators use them for billing and compliance.
• Doctors assess treatment effectiveness and interpret diagnostic data.
• Nurses stay informed about potential complications or alerts.
• Researchers extract valuable data for medical discoveries.
Modern EHR systems are decentralized and accessible from anywhere, thanks to
blockchain connectivity that links multiple hospitals. This ensures secure and consistent
access to patient data across institutions.
When compared to paper-based or traditional file systems, EHRs offer several advantages:
• Faster and more efficient data storage and retrieval.
• Enhanced accuracy in diagnoses and patient outcomes.
• Improved patient engagement in their healthcare journey.
• Better coordination among care providers.
• Significant cost reduction due to elimination of paper and physical storage.
• Greater potential for research and analytics.
